Privacy, plainly

No account is required to track items. The app stores item names, notes, dates and statuses on your device. No advertising SDK or analytics is included.

RevenueCat receives purchase-related information and an anonymous app identifier when you use purchases. It does not receive your item list. Exported backups contain your notes: share them only with people you trust.
